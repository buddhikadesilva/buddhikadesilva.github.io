Shrimp Counter

Developed an innovative Android app for counting shrimp populations in a designated area, with the added functionality of being able to count other small objects such as fish and eggs. The app allows users to easily upload images from their phone gallery or camera and classify shrimp as either 'microscope' or 'other' based on their size. Moreover, users are able to adjust the image sizes, threshold, and shrimp size to achieve accurate counts. The app has been well-received by both shrimp farming communities and researchers and has the potential to significantly improve the efficiency of shrimp population monitoring and research.
